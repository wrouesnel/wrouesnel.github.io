    Framebuffer/GRUB Console performance and Nvidia GPUs (50xx)

    wrouesnel

    2025-11-23 11:21

    I upgraded to an NVIDIA GTX Ti 5060 recently as a mid-range choice to ensure my desktop had CUDA compatibility. It's been smooth sailing on my Windows and Linux dual boot system with one problem - performance in the grub console menu was absolutely abysmmal (as in, one motion every few seconds - for both menus and typing).

    Lowering the resolution helped but did not solve the problem.

    Reading around the advice I saw was to enable "CSM" or "Legacy CSM" mode in the UEFI BIOS.

    This completely fixed the problem - the console is now fast as is the console when booted into Linux.
